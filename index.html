Why the paper matters as much as the gsm

gsm is the weight of the paper, not its thickness. Uncoated paper is bulkier than gloss, so at the same gsm an uncoated book has a spine roughly half as thick again. The calculator looks up the real thickness of each paper from the mill's published figures and does the maths for you.

Paper mills allow a small variation from batch to batch, which is why a range is shown.

What this figure leaves out

This is the thickness of the printed pages only. Endpapers, sewing, glue and the hardback boards all add to the finished spine, and the printer will add their own allowance for those. Ask them to confirm the final spine against a bulking dummy on the actual stock before artwork is finalised.

Gloss uses the published figures for Sappi Magno Gloss. Uncoated uses Arctic Paper’s Munken Lynx, a standard art-book uncoated. Some uncoated papers are made extra bulky; on those the spine can be up to about 25% wider, so check the printer’s named stock.
